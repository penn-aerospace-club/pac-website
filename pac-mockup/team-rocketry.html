The Team

First two years, second competition.

After competing at the 2024 Spaceport America Cup and the 2025 International Rocket Engineering Competition (the Cup's successor event) in our first two years back, we're building a lasting foundation for advanced rocketry at Penn. Our team is divided into subteams and values passion, dedication, and a positive attitude over prior experience.

AVIONICS

Designs, builds, and integrates the rocket's flight computer, sensors, and communications systems — collecting data and controlling onboard devices throughout the entire flight.
