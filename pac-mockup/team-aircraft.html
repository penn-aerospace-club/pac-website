The Team

Fourth place worldwide, first year competing.

As one of PAC's newer teams, Aircraft has made great strides in the last few years to design and construct multiple R/C aircraft in an effort to compete in the AIAA Design Build Fly competition. With a fourth-place ranking worldwide (of over 150 teams) on the preliminary report in the team's first ever year of competition, the group is now preparing for the fly-off.
