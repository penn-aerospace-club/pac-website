The Team

A fully custom axial micro-turbojet.

In April 2025, Penn Jet Propulsion successfully completed a fully custom-designed axial micro-turbojet engine, achieving a theoretical thrust-to-weight ratio of 3.5. This engine is the first of its kind entirely developed by undergraduate students — every major component was designed in house, most parts were manufactured internally, and final assembly was conducted entirely by the student team. Through PJP's collaborative team structure, members get hands-on experience across the full engineering design process.
