Our teams fly high.

We're divided into four sub-teams — High-Powered Rocketry, High-Altitude Balloon, Jet Propulsion, and Aircraft — each running real hardware programs.

High-Powered Rocketry
12,489 ft
SPACEPORT AMERICA CUP — JUN 2024

First competitive launch since the club's reinstatement

12 of 20 team members traveled to compete. As a rookie team, they landed with no damage and outperformed roughly 25 schools.

High-Altitude Balloon
5 payloads
POTTSTOWN, PA — NOV 2024

First flight of five new engineering projects

A custom-built payload, two solar panels, a team-developed weather-prediction ML model, and an expanded suite of weather sensors — all launched from Warwick County Park.

Jet Propulsion
3.5 T/W
COMPLETED — APR 2025

Fully custom axial micro-turbojet engine

The first of its kind entirely developed by undergraduates — every major component designed in house, most parts manufactured internally.

Aircraft

AIAA DESIGN BUILD FLY — PRELIM

Fourth place worldwide, first year competing

Our newest team designed and built multiple R/C aircraft, ranking 4th of over 150 teams on their preliminary report.
